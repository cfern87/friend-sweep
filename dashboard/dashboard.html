Scans to run

Scheduled runs open a Facebook tab and take focus while they scroll, so pick a cadence you won't mind interrupting you. Only scans are automated — friend removals always stay manual. Runs are skipped while another job is going, and only fire while Chrome is open.

Settings & safety

Removals are paced with randomized delays (never faster than ~30s) and capped per run. Automating actions is against Facebook's terms of service — going too fast can get your account temporarily action-blocked. Keep runs small (10–20) and spread them across days.
